# ADR 0030 — Night and paper: the visual identity

**Status:** accepted (2026-10-18). Amends the token and typography sections of `05-ui-guidelines.md`; no earlier ADR is superseded.

## Context

The interface was dressed in Catppuccin Mocha, a code-editor palette, with Inter alone, a sidebar, a top bar and every surface drawn as a rounded card with the same radius and the same hairline: the rail was a card, the list was a card, each row was a card, and a row carried its state as a run of chips. It was indistinguishable from an administration template and said nothing about an executive office. The maintainer's own published work is editorial: warm darks, ivory, a display face used sparingly, one marker colour, structure carried by type and spacing. The brief for the product was simpler than that work, not a copy of it: dark first, simple, sleek, elegant.

## Decision

- **Two themes, night and paper, dark by default.** Night is a cool slate near-black ground for the shell, a slate canvas one clear step lighter for the page, a third step for anything raised, edges that read at a glance, off-white ink (never pure white) and sage as the one marker. Paper is a cool grey paper ground, a near-white sheet, near-black ink and moss as the marker: the same hue taken down until it clears 4.5:1 on the sheet. A cool marker on slate darks leaves the overdue red as the only warm thing on a page, which is where the eye should go; the warm brass the first draft tried competed with it. Every text token clears 4.5:1 on the surface it sits on and on the raised step; every mark clears 3:1; the scorecard status scale keeps its `-ink` variants for the same reason as before.
- **The primary action is ink on the ground**, `--color-primary` mapped to the text token and its foreground to the ground, in both themes. The accent is a marker: the current view, the current row, the current destination's icon, a count, a link. A filled button in it would be the loudest thing on every page, ahead of whatever is late.
- **A named type scale.** The sizes the identity adds between Tailwind's own steps are tokens in `tokens.css` (`text-meta` 13 px, `text-nav` 15 px, `text-brand` 22 px, `text-heading` 26 px, `text-title` 28 px), never a pixel value written into a component. Arabic navigation labels take `text-nav`, one step above the Latin ones, because Arabic letterforms sit smaller on the line.
- **The shell's one flourish.** A faint radial wash of the marker hue falls from behind the brand and is gone before the first destination ends, defined once on `.app-sidebar` from the ground and accent tokens, so it follows both themes.
- **A display pair for the places a page is read by.** Newsreader (Latin) and Noto Naskh Arabic, loaded through `next/font` like the text faces and ordered by direction the same way, under one `--font-display` token. It carries the page title in the entity bar, the day's headline on Home, the headings a grouped list is organised under and the featured counts. It never carries a row, a label or a control, and never appears below 18 px. Inter and IBM Plex Sans Arabic carry everything else, unchanged.
- **Structure is felt, not drawn.** The rail and the list sit flush on the page canvas. Grouped facts sit on an inset sheet, a step darker than the canvas with a full edge: a record's properties, Home's week, and each card in a card list, so the card view and the table read as two instruments rather than one with and one without column headings. A scorecard tile is the other bordered surface in a list (EP-B27); the one raised surface on a page is the open record, which now also casts the slide-over shadow (EP-B26). "You are here" is said once per surface. In a list it is a 3 px bar on the start edge, mirrored in RTL, the same device for the views rail and the current row. The shell's navigation is the one place it is a contained pill: the raised ground, a fine inset edge and the destination's icon in the marker hue, because a bar on a 240 px rail of icon-and-label rows reads as a stray line rather than a selection. Hover is a lighter wash in both, never the selected look.
- **A state travels as ink.** A group heading takes the tone its group carries (`groupTone`: danger for what is late, accent for what is today); a priority is a word with a dot in the danger or warning ink for urgent and high and nothing for the rest; a status is plain text. No chips, pills or tinted boxes on a row. Uppercase tracked labels are gone from the rail and the headings.

## Consequences

- `tokens.css` is the whole palette change; no TSX file names a colour (the lint rule stands). Chart colours resolve the same semantic variables at paint time and follow without change.
- The entity framework's card presentation (EP-B20) is redrawn once for every module that uses it: Tasks, Notes and Committees share the line, the heading and the quiet featured readings. Modules opt into a heading tone with `groupTone`; without it a heading is ink.
- The a11y audit's contrast floors are unchanged and the new tokens are chosen to meet them; the browser suite is the proof.
- The Home headline and the entity bar title change face and size and nothing else about them; their copy, behaviour and tests stand.
- Catppuccin Mocha is no longer referenced anywhere; a later theme choice is a new ADR, not an edit to this one.
