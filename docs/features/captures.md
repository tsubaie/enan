# Feature: Captures

**Status:** accepted
**Spec reviewed:** 2026-11-22
**Implementation verified:** not yet
**Owner module:** `src/modules/captures`

## Purpose

Let a member speak a note anywhere in the app and review what it becomes before anything goes live. A recording is saved as a capture, transcribed onto the capture, and interpreted into one or more drafts (a task, a meeting note or a committee note). Each draft is approved, edited, discarded or undone by the person who recorded it. Nothing is created until Approve (ADR 0071), and nobody but the recorder hears of a recording before it: one grouped line when drafts are ready and one when a recording could not be processed (ADR 0073).

## Concepts and vocabulary

Capture (one recording with its transcript and processing state), Draft or suggestion (one proposed task or note from a capture), Field state (`confident`, `several`, `unknown`: how sure the interpreter is of one field, shown in words, never as a percentage), Source span (character offsets of the sentence a draft came from, inside the capture's transcript), Type flag (a non-blocking "looks like a …" hint when the kind is uncertain), Live record (the task or note Approve created), Review (the inbox of drafts, labelled «مراجعة» / "Review").

A meeting note is an ordinary note with a type, a date and an optional committee; a committee note is an ordinary note with a required committee. There is no meetings module and no committee roster in v1.

## Data model

See `03-data-model.md` § captures, capture_suggestions. Invariants:

- CAPTURES-I01 `captures` and `capture_suggestions` are additive tables (migration `0021_captures`, ADR 0050). `duration_seconds` is 0..900; `captures.status` is one of `queued`, `transcribing`, `interpreting`, `ready`, `needs_transcript`, `failed`; a suggestion's `kind` is `task`, `meeting_note` or `committee_note`, its `status` `pending`, `approved`, `discarded` or `merged`, and its `live_entity_type` `task` or `note` (CHECKs listing every Zod option). The audio is a `files` row the capture holds with `ON DELETE RESTRICT`; suggestions are owned children (`ON DELETE CASCADE`). The migration copies no `note_voice_memos` row into `captures` and rewrites no note: existing memos stay on the notes they belong to.

Payload shapes, per kind (Zod, stored as JSON in `payload`):

| Kind | Required for Approve | Optional |
|---|---|---|
| `task` | `title` | `ownerId` (an assignable person), `spokenOwner`, `dueDate`, `committeeId`, `description`, `priority`; `status` is always `inbox` |
| `meeting_note` | `title`, `type`, `noteDate`, `body` | `committeeId`, `participantIds`, `tags` |
| `committee_note` | `title`, `committeeId`, `noteDate`, `body` | `type`, `participantIds`, `tags` |

A required field may be held as null while it is unresolved; a field whose state is `unknown`, or `several` without a choice, blocks Approve. An unmatched optional owner does not: the reviewer chooses one or clears it.

## Behaviors

- CAPTURES-B01 A recording stores its audio first (ADR 0054), then inserts a capture row. It creates no note and no task until Approve. AI off, unconfigured or over budget leaves the capture `needs_transcript` with its audio kept. After Stop the recorder closes at once and the reader stays where they were; there is no sheet after Stop (CAPTURES-B20).
- CAPTURES-B02 A capture, its suggestions and its audio are visible only to the member who recorded it (`created_by`) and to administrators, on every read, list, count and mutation. Another member, a missing id and a deleted capture all answer `not_found` with `{ entityType: 'capture' }`, never `forbidden`. Lists and counts filter through the same visibility predicate as single reads. The review count is the reader's own pending drafts plus own failed captures, administrators included; administrators reach other members' drafts through an inbox filter.
- CAPTURES-B03 Before Approve the only notifications are the recorder's own `captures.ready` and `captures.failed` (CAPTURES-B21, ADR 0073). Recording, transcription, interpretation, editing, discarding, deleting and restoring emit nothing else, and a capture's jobs never write `job.finished`. Approve fires only the ordinary side effects of the record it creates (`task.assigned`, `note.mentioned`).
- CAPTURES-B04 Approve never starts refinement and never passes `autoApply`; refining the resulting note stays a manual action.
- CAPTURES-B05 Audio is never attached to an existing note: no automatic write, no manual attach, no in-note recorder. Captures never enqueue `ai.notes.transcribe`.
- CAPTURES-B06 Existing voice memos stay playable (ranged), downloadable and deletable on their notes; a memo that never got text may still be retried with AI or given a typed transcript.
- CAPTURES-B07 `POST /api/v1/notes/voice` and `POST /api/v1/notes/:id/voice` answer 410 `gone` `{ reason: "retired", use: "/api/v1/captures" }` for new audio, in the same release as the review inbox, and create no note and no memo. Both answer before the body is read: `POST /notes/voice` checks `captures.inbox` first, so a missing, invalid or valid upload alike gets 410 and nothing is buffered. The workspace setting `captures.inbox` (boolean, default `true`; an administrator writes it through `PATCH /admin/settings`) is the post-release rollback: off, `POST /notes/voice` creates a note from a recording again (NOTES-B36), with no refinement and no attach; `POST /notes/:id/voice` stays 410 and the notes-list strip does not come back. The capture tables stay either way. The rollback records too, so it also answers 403 `forbidden` `{ reason: "recording_off", scope }` while recording is off (CAPTURES-B14), checked after `captures.inbox` and still before the body is read.
- CAPTURES-B08 Listen plays the whole recording and highlights the draft's source sentence in the transcript (the provider returns no segment timestamps).
- CAPTURES-B09 Field states are `confident`, `several` or `unknown`, shown as text and icon, never as a percentage or by colour alone.
- CAPTURES-B10 Approve creates the live task or note from the edited payload in one transaction, through the owning module's own create (`createTask`, `createNote`), then records the live pointer, the live record's revision, `approved_at` and `approved_by` on the draft. It requires the draft's `revision` and every required field settled (422 `rule_violation` `{ reason: "unresolved", fields }` otherwise); a type flag never blocks. A second Approve with the same revision answers with the first live record and creates nothing. Approve N ready approves, in one transaction, exactly the listed drafts (422 `{ reason: "not_ready", id }` if one is not ready, and nothing is approved) or, without ids, the reader's own ready drafts, skipping the rest. An edit (`PATCH`) is checked against its kind's contract and settles the fields it sets; a changed kind keeps the shared fields (a task's description is a note's body). Discard needs no confirmation. Merge joins drafts of one recording and one kind: the kept draft takes the others' source sentences and the others become `merged`; across kinds it is 422 `{ reason: "kind" }`. A task draft's `source_note_id` stays empty: sibling drafts are not linked. In the inbox a field saves when it is left; Approve or Discard pressed while that save is being sent waits for it, busy and with «Saving your change…» in words, then is sent once with the revision the save answered; if the save fails nothing is sent and the failure is shown. An edit made while another is being saved is not dropped: edits made meanwhile are merged (the latest value of each field and the latest kind win) into one `PATCH` sent after the one in flight, with the revision it answered, and Approve or Discard waits for that one too; if the first fails the merged edit is not sent. Approve N ready waits for every save being sent, reads the list again and approves the drafts ready in it, leaving out any whose latest save failed. Its toast adds, at high priority, how many drafts ready at the click were left out because their last change did not save («استُبعدت مسودة واحدة لأن آخر تعديل عليها لم يُحفظ، وما زالت في المراجعة.») and how many were no longer ready («ما زالت مسودة واحدة تحتاج إلى اختيار، فبقيت في المراجعة.»), and focus goes to the first draft left out; with nothing left to approve it sends nothing and says «لم تُعتمد أي مسودة. راجع المسودات المشار إليها ثم أعد المحاولة.» / "No drafts were approved. Check the drafts marked in Review, then try again." at high priority.
- CAPTURES-B11 Undo of an approval soft-deletes the live record (its module's delete, with a new `deleted_op_id`) and returns the draft to pending only while the live record's revision still equals the one recorded at Approve and within 60 seconds of Approve; otherwise it answers 422 `rule_violation` `{ reason: "undo_unavailable" }` and the record stays. Undo of a discard returns the draft to pending. Undoing an Approve N ready batch (`POST /captures/suggestions/undo` with its ids) is one transaction, all or nothing. Notifications the approval already sent stay sent.
- CAPTURES-B12 Uploads are capped at 25 MB and checked by their magic bytes, not their name or declared type; a recording is at most 15 minutes (900 seconds). The server reads the length from the container header (WebM Segment Info Duration or last Cluster, MP4 `mvhd`, Ogg last granule, WAV data over byte rate, MP3 Xing frame count or constant bitrate), with no decoder and no subprocess. A file its header proves longer than 900 seconds is refused with `422 rule_violation {reason: 'too_long', maxSeconds: 900}`, whatever the client declared. The capture stores, and transcription reserves for, the longer of the declared and the measured length; a file whose header gives no length is estimated at 16 kbps of its size, at most 900 seconds. Reading a header takes at most 10,000 steps (elements, boxes, chunks or scan candidates); a header that needs more gives no length, so a 25 MB file of tiny parts cannot stall the server. Each refusal names its reason (`too_large`, `unsupported_type`, `too_long`), and the client says it in the person's words as a high-priority toast; a refused file chosen from S3 reopens S3 with the reason inline (`role="alert"`) and focus on Upload audio, and a refused recording stays held with Retry.
- CAPTURES-B13 Deleting a recording (`DELETE /captures/:id`) soft-deletes the capture and its drafts still live under one operation id; the audio `files` row and binary stay (ADR 0034) and live records an earlier Approve made stay as they are. A repeat delete by the recorder or an administrator answers 204 with the first delete's `X-Op-Id` and rewrites nothing. `POST /captures/:id/restore` `{ opId }` brings back the capture and exactly the drafts carrying that operation id, and is audited. It runs in one `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction, and a job's publish reads the fence and writes its result inside its own, so neither lands between the other's read and write. A capture restored while still waiting on a step (queued, transcribing, interpreting) whose job is over, because it finished while the capture was deleted and its result was fenced off, is set to failed with `error_code` `interrupted`, so Retry and Type the transcript take it, and its card says «توقفت المعالجة عند حذف هذا التسجيل. أعد المحاولة لإعداد مسوداته.» / "Processing stopped when this recording was deleted. Try again to prepare its drafts."; the restore's audit details then read `{ reconciled: true, previousStatus }`. A job still queued or running is left to finish, and one that failed keeps its own reason. A repeat restore with the same `opId` answers 200 with the capture and writes nothing. Anyone else, a missing id, a wrong `opId` or a capture that is not deleted answer 404 `not_found` `{ entityType: "capture" }`, never `forbidden`. Neither delete nor restore emits a notification (CAPTURES-B03).
- CAPTURES-B14 **Recording off for the workspace.** The workspace setting `captures.enabled` (boolean, default `true`; an administrator writes it through `PATCH /admin/settings`, audited, ADMIN-B77) turns recording on or off for every member; it is not the `captures.inbox` hatch (CAPTURES-B07) and not the AI capability `captures.interpret`, whose being off never hides a record control. Recording is on for a reader only while `captures.enabled` and their own `user.captures.enabled` (CAPTURES-B15) are both on. While it is off for them, `POST /captures` (and the rollback `POST /notes/voice`) answers 403 `forbidden` `{ reason: "recording_off", scope: "workspace" | "account" }` before the body is read, so no audio is buffered or stored; a failed settings read fails the request rather than letting it through. Both routes check again once the body has arrived and before the audio is stored, and once more inside the transaction that writes the rows, so a switch turned off while a body is still arriving refuses it the same way and stores neither a file nor a row. Only a switch turned off in the narrow moment between the pre-store check and the in-transaction check can leave a stored audio file with no `files` row; such unreferenced voice files are for a scheduled sweep ([#186](https://github.com/tsubaie/enan/issues/186)), never deleted inline, since identical uploads share one content-addressed file. Nothing else is gated: list, count, detail, audio, Type the transcript, Retry, delete, restore and every review route keep their rules, and nothing is deleted. The shell is told on the server and draws no phone record button, no header «تسجيل ملاحظة» / "Record a note" and no recorder or Alt+V, while Review and its panel stay; the empty inbox offers no Record. While the workspace switch is off the inbox shows a note (`role="note"`): «التسجيل متوقف من مسؤول مساحة العمل» / "Recording is turned off by your workspace admin" and «ما زال بإمكانك مراجعة المسودات الحالية واعتمادها أو تجاهلها.» / "You can still review, approve or discard the drafts you already have." A refused upload says «التسجيل متوقف من مسؤول مساحة العمل.» / "Recording is turned off by your workspace admin." A settings table that cannot be read draws the built-in defaults (recording on); that is for drawing only.
- CAPTURES-B15 **The reader's own switches.** `user.captures.enabled` and `user.captures.shortcut` (booleans, default `true`, user-scoped) are written only by the reader through `PATCH /account` (ACCT-B11). Off, `user.captures.enabled` hides the reader's record controls and refuses their audio as CAPTURES-B14 does (`scope: "account"`), without the workspace note; `user.captures.shortcut` off removes Alt+V and every mention of it (CAPTURES-B19). Both are kept while the workspace switch is off and apply once it is back on. A save re-renders the shell from the server.
- CAPTURES-B16 **Where the record control lives.** Two workspace settings (administrators only, through `PATCH /admin/settings`, audited, ADMIN-B77) hold the default: `captures.default_mobile_placement` (`floating-end` | `floating-start` | `more`, default `floating-end`) and `captures.default_desktop_placement` (`header` | `account-menu` | `hidden`, default `header`). Three user settings hold the reader's own choice, written only by the reader through `PATCH /account` (ACCT-B11): `user.captures.mobile_placement` (`workspace` | `floating` | `more`), `user.captures.floating_side` (`end` | `start`, read only with `floating`) and `user.captures.desktop_placement` (`workspace` | `header` | `account-menu` | `hidden`). `workspace` is never stored: writing it clears the reader's row (ACCT-I03), so a reader on the workspace default follows every later change to it, while a stored row is always a concrete choice that a later workspace change does not move. Each is resolved on the server per request, the reader's row first, then the workspace default, then the built-in one (`floating-end`, `header`; also what an unreadable settings table draws), and handed to the shell and the inbox as props (no context, ADR 0072). Phone and computer are told apart by the 1024 px breakpoint in CSS only; both are drawn and one is shown. A member learns the workspace defaults only through `GET /account` (`captures.workspaceMobile`, `workspaceDesktop`); `GET /admin/settings` stays an administrator's. `captures.inbox` plays no part.
- CAPTURES-B17 **On a phone.** While recording is on, exactly one way to record is drawn below 1024 px. Floating: the plain 56 px microphone circle (S1, «تسجيل ملاحظة» / "Record a note"), one tap starts recording, at the logical end (`end-4`: bottom-left in Arabic, bottom-right in English) or start, above the navigation capsule on every signed-in screen; no pill, chevron, long-press menu, shrinking or scroll-hiding. It is not drawn while a recording is active, and is hidden while a record's sheet hides the bar (`.entity-page-open`) and while a text field, select or editable content is focused on a touch device (the on-screen keyboard); a toast stands above it only while it is shown. In the More menu: no circle; «تسجيل ملاحظة» / "Record a note" is the first action of the More sheet (an action, not a destination; the bar keeps four destinations and More), with the reader's pending count (the Review badge's number) as «{count} مسودات لك بانتظار المراجعة» / "{count} of your drafts are waiting for review" and a count circle; its click closes the sheet and starts the recorder in the same event. The More tab then carries a dot («مسودات بانتظار المراجعة» / "Drafts waiting for review" to a screen reader) only while that count is above zero, and is where focus returns when a recorder started from the sheet closes without sending. There is no Hidden on a phone.
- CAPTURES-B18 **On a computer, and in the inbox.** From 1024 px: Header button, the existing «تسجيل ملاحظة» / "Record a note" outline button with its Alt+V keycap (S9); In the account menu, no header button and «تسجيل ملاحظة» / "Record a note" as the top action of the avatar's menu, above Account, with the pending count, a count circle and the Alt+V keycap while the shortcut is on; its click closes the menu and starts the recorder, and the avatar is where focus returns (on a computer only: on a phone the avatar holds no Record and is passed over for the More tab); Hidden, neither, Alt+V alone (while the shortcut is on). Review and the bell stay in the header in every placement. The inbox header (page and panel) shows «تسجيل» / "Record" beside «اعتماد N جاهزة» / "Approve N ready" whenever recording is on, drafts or none, at every width; with Hidden and the shortcut on it is preceded, on a computer, by «اضغط Alt+V للتسجيل» / "Press Alt+V to record". The empty inbox keeps its own Record (S16).
- CAPTURES-B19 **Alt+V.** One `keydown` listener on the document, mounted only while recording and the shortcut are on for the reader, at every width. It starts recording on `code === "KeyV"` with Alt and without Ctrl, Meta or Shift (so Option+V on a Mac, «ر» on an Arabic layout and never Windows AltGr, which arrives as Ctrl+Alt), and calls `preventDefault` so Firefox's View menu does not open. It ignores a held repeat (`event.repeat`), IME composition (`event.isComposing` or `keyCode` 229) and a target that is an input, a textarea, a select or inside editable content. With the shortcut off no control carries `aria-keyshortcuts` or an Alt+V keycap.

- CAPTURES-B20 **After Stop.** Each recording becomes an item of the tab's outbox, its audio held in memory only (never localStorage or IndexedDB), and one toast follows it, updated in place; toasts never take focus, and focus returns to the opener only if it was lost. Uploading: «جارٍ رفع التسجيل…» / "Uploading recording…" with an indeterminate progress bar (`role="progressbar"`, no value: the upload reports no progress), no timeout. Accepted and processing, the first time on this device (versioned `enan.captures.first-run:v1`, read and written in the event path, set only when shown): «حُفظ التسجيل. نُعدّ مسوداته في الخلفية» / "Recording saved. We're preparing its drafts in the background.", "Carry on working; we'll let you know when they're ready." and the S30 privacy line, with «كيف يعمل هذا؟» / "How it works" (to `/captures`) and Close, until closed. Later ones: «جارٍ تجهيز تسجيلك في الخلفية» / "Processing your recording in the background" with «عرض» / "View" and Close, for five seconds (held while hovered or focused). Saved without a transcript (`needs_transcript`): a warning-tone toast «حُفظ التسجيل، لكن التفريغ غير متاح الآن» / "Recording saved, but transcription isn't available right now" with View and Close, until closed; it leaves the first-run toast for the next processed recording. At most one of these two persistent notices at a time; a newer one closes the older. Upload failed (offline, a server error, a full queue): an alert «تعذّر رفع التسجيل» / "Couldn't upload the recording", "It's kept on this device until you retry.", with «تجاهل» / Discard and «إعادة المحاولة» / Retry (the same file), until answered. Refused (too large, too long, unsupported, recording off): an alert with the reason and Discard only. A file from the refused-microphone sheet that is refused reopens the sheet with the reason instead (CAPTURES-B12). While an item is uploading or held after a failure the browser warns before leaving the page. Ready in the same session raises no toast. At most two toasts show app-wide; an older one waits hidden and comes back when a newer one closes. The delete receipt and these toasts never close each other.
- CAPTURES-B21 **The recorder hears when drafts are ready** (ADR 0073, NOTIF-B18). When an interpretation publishes a capture `ready`, its recorder's single unread `captures.ready` line is written or moved to the top (one line per recorder, subject = the recorder's user id); its number is the reader's own live captures that are ready with a pending draft, counted when the bell is read, so Approve, Discard, Delete and Restore keep it right without a write, and at zero it is not drawn. It resolves only for its own recorder, an administrator included. When the job a capture waits on fails for good, or the interpretation its transcription asked for is refused inside that job, the recorder gets a `captures.failed` line for that capture, opening `/captures?capture=<id>` (the inbox focuses that recording once listed); it resolves while the reader may see the capture and it still reads as failed. Both are written in the job's own transaction behind the same fence as its result: a deleted capture, one waiting on another job, a retryable failure, a restore, and any request (Retry, Type the transcript) write nothing. No actor, and nothing of the recording in the payload.
## API

All routes take the session guard plus the module's own visibility check (CAPTURES-B02); a draft is reached through its capture, so another member's, a missing and a deleted one answer the same 404.

| Method | Path | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `POST` | `/captures` | multipart `file`, `durationSeconds` 0..900, optional `transcript`, `committeeId`, `type`; 201 with the capture. AI off, unconfigured or over budget does not fail it (`needs_transcript`); 403 `recording_off` `{ scope: 'workspace' \| 'account' }` before the body is read (CAPTURES-B14); 422 `too_large` / `unsupported_type`; 429 `{ scope: 'queue' }` |
| `GET` | `/captures` | `scope` (`mine`, the default, or `all`: everyone's for an administrator, the reader's own for a member), `cursor`, `limit` (≤ 50); captures newest first, each with its drafts and `audioUrl` |
| `GET` | `/captures/count` | `{ data: { pending } }` for the review badge |
| `GET` | `/captures/:id` | one capture |
| `GET` | `/captures/:id/audio` | the audio, 200 or 206 by `Range` (416 outside it), `Cache-Control: private, no-store`; 404 for anyone who may not see the capture |
| `POST` | `/captures/:id/transcript`, `/transcribe`, `/interpret` | a typed transcript (then interpretation), a transcription retry, an interpretation retry; 409 while a job runs, 422 `no_transcript`, 503 when AI cannot take a retry |
| `DELETE` | `/captures/:id` | 204 with `X-Op-Id`; soft-deletes the capture and its drafts under one operation id; the binary is kept (ADR 0034); a repeat by the recorder or an admin is 204 with the same op id (CAPTURES-B13) |
| `POST` | `/captures/:id/restore` | `{ opId }`; the capture with its drafts (failed as `interrupted` if the job it waited on ended meanwhile); a repeat with the same op id answers the capture again; 404 for a wrong op id, someone else, or a capture not deleted (CAPTURES-B13) |
| `PATCH` | `/captures/suggestions/:id` | `{ revision, kind?, payload }`; the draft; 400 a field its kind refuses, 409 stale revision or not pending |
| `POST` | `/captures/suggestions/:id/approve` | `{ revision }`; `{ suggestion, live: { type, id } }`; 409 stale or not pending, 422 unresolved |
| `POST` | `/captures/suggestions/approve-ready` | `{ ids? }`; `{ approved: [{ suggestion, live }] }`; 422 `not_ready` |
| `POST` | `/captures/suggestions/:id/discard` | `{ revision }`; the draft, `discarded` |
| `POST` | `/captures/suggestions/:id/undo`, `/captures/suggestions/undo` | one draft, or `{ ids }` for a batch; 422 `undo_unavailable` |
| `POST` | `/captures/:id/merge` | `{ keepId, mergeIds }`; the kept draft; 422 `kind` |

## UI

- Entry: where the reader's placement puts it (CAPTURES-B16–B18). On a phone, the plain 56 px microphone circle at the logical end or start above the bar (S1), or «تسجيل ملاحظة» / "Record a note" as the first action of the More sheet with the More tab's dot; on a computer, «تسجيل ملاحظة» / "Record a note" with an Alt+V keycap in the header (S9; tooltip «على macOS: ⌥V» / "⌥V on macOS"), the top action of the account menu, or nothing but the chord; and «تسجيل» / "Record" in the inbox header whenever recording is on. The variants are one compound component (`CaptureEntry.*`) and one pure mapping (`captureEntries`) that hands the shell's layout `ReactNode` slots; the layout knows nothing of captures. Alt/Option+V is registered once in the shell, is a modifier chord only, and is ignored while focus is in a field or text is being composed (CAPTURES-B19). With recording off none of these is drawn (CAPTURES-B14). Started on a committee page (`/committees?id=…`) the capture carries that committee, shown as a clearable chip.
- First start only: S2 asks before the browser does and says that the audio is sent to be transcribed when transcription is on (remembered on the device). A refused microphone (S3) offers an upload of M4A, MP3, WAV, WebM or OGG instead.
- The recorder is full screen on a phone and a floating card on a desktop, both on the solid `--elevated` surface so nothing behind shows through in any theme. On a phone it is a modal dialog: the page and the bottom bar are inert while it is open, so Tab stays inside it. Tap to start; Pause/Resume; Stop. Cancel within 5 seconds discards at once, after 5 seconds it asks (S6). On a desktop Escape pauses and offers Stop and save, Keep recording (the default, focused) and Discard recording (S10). From one minute a non-blocking warning shows (S7); a second before 15:00 (899 seconds, so encoder padding cannot carry a full-length recording past the 900-second cap) the recording stops and is saved with a toast (S8). The clock, the meter and the microphone do not mirror in RTL.
- After Stop (S11): no sheet. The recorder closes and the reader stays where they were; toasts say what became of the recording, stacked above the phone circle (at End or Start) or the bar, two at most (CAPTURES-B20). Ready drafts reach the bell as one grouped line (CAPTURES-B21).
- The header's «مراجعة» / "Review" carries the count of the reader's own pending drafts and failed captures (S13). On a desktop it opens Review as a side panel beside the page, with Open as a page and Close; on a phone, and as a link, it is the `/captures` page (read on the server for the reader, then kept fresh on the client). Administrators have Show everyone's drafts (`?scope=all`).
- Review (S14) says how many drafts wait and offers «اعتماد ٣ جاهزة» / "Approve 3 ready" (S23) for every shown draft with nothing required left, type flags included; with none it reads «لا مسودات جاهزة بعد» / "No drafts are ready yet". The first visit shows S30's line, «المسودات يراها من سجّلها والمسؤولون فقط، ولا تُعتمد إلا بطلبك.» / "Only you and admins can see drafts. Nothing goes live until you approve.", until it is dismissed on that device. Empty (S16): «لا مسودات بانتظار المراجعة.» with Record a note and its Alt+V keycap.

## AI (if any)

Job kinds `ai.captures.transcribe` (the existing `notes.transcribe` capability, budget and `ai.model.transcription` slot, admitted on the capture's own kind; dedup `captures.transcribe:<captureId>`) and `ai.captures.interpret` (capability `captures.interpret`, the same text slot as `notes.refine`; dedup `captures.interpret:<captureId>:<transcriptHash>`). The interpreter receives the transcript and a small catalogue as framed data: active committees (id, name), assignable people (id, name), enabled note types and today. The transcript is untrusted data, never instructions; the output is Zod-validated, ids outside the catalogue are dropped (an unmatched spoken owner is kept as words with field state `unknown`), a source sentence not found in the transcript gives no span, an unsure meeting type stays unset and `unknown`, and nothing it returns is executed. Interpretation replaces the pending drafts and sets the capture `ready`; no valid draft fails it with `interpret_failed`. A capture reads as `failed`, with the job's reason, once the job it waits on has failed or been cancelled. Neither job writes a note or a task, writes `job.finished`, or starts refinement; the only notifications they write are the recorder's `captures.ready` and `captures.failed` (CAPTURES-B21).

## Audit items

- Every capture path calls `assertCaptureAccess` or filters by `visibleCaptures`.
- No capture code path enqueues `ai.notes.transcribe` or writes into a note before Approve, and the only notifications it emits are `captures.ready` and `captures.failed`, to the recorder, from a job's publish or fail transaction behind its fence (ADR 0073).
- `captures.ready` resolves only for `ctx.user.id` and counts only the reader's own captures; `captures.failed` resolves through `canViewCapture`; neither payload carries text from the recording.
- Held audio never leaves the tab except to `POST /captures` and is never written to storage; Discard drops it.

## Out of scope

Meetings records and committee rosters; attaching audio to existing notes; segment-level audio seek; a dedicated upload rate limiter beyond the size, duration and queue limits.
